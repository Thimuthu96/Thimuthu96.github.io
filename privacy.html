Privacy Policy

Tiny Whys Uploader · Last updated 6 October 2026

Who uses this app

Tiny Whys Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Tiny Whys YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

The app requests one Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store any other data from Google accounts, and it does not access any other user's account.

How data is stored

The authorisation token for the owner's account is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only to upload videos. Video titles, descriptions and upload dates are logged in that repository.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
